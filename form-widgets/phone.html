Run it in the demo app

Every example on this page is live in Amber at /reference/form-widgets/phone, where you can change it and save it. Spin up the demo app, or browse the page's source.

Phone ​

The phone field takes a phone number with a country picker at its start. It formats the number as it is typed, using the numbering rules of Google's libphonenumber (libphonenumber-js), and saves it in the international E.164 format, such as +61412345678: a plus, the country code and the number, with no spaces. That form means the same thing in every country, sorts and searches cleanly, and is the form SMS and calling services expect. The rules load the first time a page shows a phone field.

Phone number ​

A number typed without a country code belongs to defaultCountry, so 0412 345 678 in Australia saves as +61412345678. Typing a number that starts with + picks its country as you type, and once the field loses focus the number shows in that country's own format. A stored number shows in the format of its country, with that country picked.

yaml
fields:
    mobile:
        label: Mobile
        type: phone
        defaultCountry: AU
        comment: Shows 0412 345 678 and saves +61412345678. Type a number starting with + to switch country.

The cursor stays beside the same digit as the spaces and brackets move, and Backspace after a space deletes the digit before it. When the field loses focus, a number with the wrong length for its country is marked invalid. That check is the length only, so for a stricter rule, such as only Australian mobile numbers, validate the number on the model, for example with the phone rule of the laravel-phone package.

Without defaultCountry, the field takes the region of the app locale, such as AU for en_AU, and otherwise the region of the browser's language.

Choosing the countries ​

The picker shows the country's flag and calling code, and opens a list of every country with its flag, its name in the page's language and its calling code. countries limits the list to a set of two letter codes, and preferredCountries puts some at the top, above a divider. The country of a stored number is always listed, so the number still shows correctly.

yaml
fields:
    office_phone:
        label: Office Phone
        type: phone
        defaultCountry: AU
        countries: [AU, NZ, GB, US, CA]
        preferredCountries: [AU, NZ]
        comment: Five countries, with Australia and New Zealand first.

The list is Bootstrap's combobox, so it needs the Bootstrap script on the page, like the library's dropdowns. It opens at the current country, the arrow keys move through it, typing a country's name jumps to it (spaces included, as in "united k"), and Enter or Space picks the country and moves to the number.

Read-only ​

A readOnly field shows the number with the picker locked, and still posts its value, so saving the form keeps it. A disabled field looks the same but posts nothing.

yaml
fields:
    support_phone:
        label: Support Line
        type: phone
        readOnly: true
        comment: Shown in its own country's format, with the picker locked.

Preview mode ​

In preview mode the number is a link that calls it, in the national format when it belongs to the default country and in the international format otherwise.

yaml
fields:
    mobile:
        label: Mobile
        type: phone
        span: left
        defaultCountry: AU

    support_phone:
        label: Support Line
        type: phone
        span: right
        defaultCountry: AU

Saving ​

The field saves the E.164 number that the browser worked out. A number posted in the international format with spaces or punctuation, such as +61 (412) 345-678, saves as +61412345678, and an empty field saves as null. Anything else saves as posted, for the model's validation to judge.

Options ​

OptionDescription
defaultCountrythe two letter country for numbers typed without a country code. Default: the region of the app locale, otherwise of the browser's language.
countriesthe two letter countries the picker offers. Default: every country.
preferredCountriesthe two letter countries listed first in the picker.

The field also takes the common field properties, such as readOnly, disabled and placeholder.
